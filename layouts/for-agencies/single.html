About Me

A designer who's sat on both sides of the table.

I earned my BFA at the Savannah College of Art and Design, then started my career designing for corporate clients in New York before going freelance and working remotely as a digital nomad.

Since then, I've served as Creative Director at a SaaS startup through its acquisition, run a creative agency of my own, and joined tea Protocol as one of its first ten employees. That mix means I understand both sides of the agency relationship: what your clients expect, and the pressure of delivering it on deadline.

Today I partner with agencies and marketing teams who need experienced design capacity without the overhead of another hire.
